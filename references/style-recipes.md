# Approved style recipes

Use this file only after a built-in style is selected. Treat the corresponding approved sample deck and montage from `assets/styles/catalog.json` as the visual acceptance target. Reuse principles and page silhouettes; do not copy third-party artwork.

## Shared fidelity gate

1. Inspect the approved montage at full size before authoring.
2. Open the approved sample PPTX when exact spacing, type scale, chart treatment, or page furniture is unclear.
3. Pick three or more page silhouettes from the selected recipe. Do not run every slide through one generic title-and-cards layout.
4. Generate or source the primary visual before arranging the page. A missing hero asset is not permission to replace it with decorative boxes.
5. Compare the final montage with the approved montage. Reject the result when another built-in style could be obtained merely by swapping colors.

## `business-minimal` — 瑞士编辑式商务系统

**Design thesis:** clarity comes from visible order, strong reading sequence, and controlled contrast—not from reducing every page to empty space.

- Palette: warm paper white, true black, signal red. Use red for indexing, the key number, or one structural edge.
- Type: bold grotesk headlines, compact labels, small editorial metadata. Prefer short line breaks that create blocks.
- Grid: 12-column Swiss grid with vertical spines, hairlines, and deliberate alignment across distant elements.
- Media: one high-contrast monochrome architectural or workplace image; crop tightly enough to reveal structure.
- Icons: rare, monoline, geometric, black/red only.
- Page silhouettes:
  - `indexed-cover`: warm-white text field plus a red/black vertical issue spine.
  - `split-thesis`: paired claims separated by one horizontal rule and large negative space.
  - `evidence-chart`: one red lead metric at left and a black editable chart at right.
  - `monochrome-hero`: two-thirds black-and-white image, one-third black text panel, thin red divider.
- Avoid blue corporate gradients, pill components, equal-weight cards, and stock handshake imagery.

## `consulting-data` — 2+5，以现代咨询为主

**Design thesis:** make the executive conclusion visible before the supporting evidence, using modern editorial confidence rather than traditional consulting ornament.

- Palette: soft white, black, fluorescent lime. Lime is a decision signal, not a general background color on every page.
- Type: oversized uppercase conclusions and dense, precise annotations.
- Grid: modular executive grid; combine full-width bands with compact evidence cells.
- Media: credible candid executive photography with cool neutral grading.
- Icons/diagrams: circular markers, radar rings, or decision nodes only when they encode sequence or priority.
- Page silhouettes:
  - `photo-band-cover`: documentary photo strip, lime information band, large title field.
  - `lime-summary`: white evidence strip above a large lime conclusion field.
  - `black-performance`: three lead metrics, editable black-field chart, short synthesis at right.
  - `action-radar`: next-step headline paired with a sparse editable decision diagram.
- Chart rule: highlight one series or terminal value in lime; mute everything else.
- Avoid default red consulting rules, ornamental infographics, and grids of many equal cards.

## `tech-dark` — 1+5

**Design thesis:** combine cinematic autonomous-system imagery with the precision of an instrument panel.

- Palette: deep near-black, cool graphite, cyan telemetry signal. Keep 80% or more of the canvas dark.
- Type: bold engineering headlines, compact system labels, small coordinate-like metadata.
- Grid: cinematic split with one large focal image or one telemetry surface.
- Media: realistic robotics, deep-sea, aerospace, or autonomous-system scenes with volumetric cyan light.
- Icons: one coherent family of thin technical line icons; use cyan only for active signal points.
- Page silhouettes:
  - `cinematic-hero`: large generated system/object visual with title anchored in black negative space.
  - `sensor-split`: method steps on a dark column, scanning environment image on the remaining field.
  - `telemetry-chart`: circular sensor icon and metrics at left, editable cyan line chart at right.
  - `capability-strip`: four technical capabilities in a horizontal system diagram.
- Avoid purple SaaS gradients, multicolor cyberpunk glow, fake UI screens, and rounded cards.

## `fresh-creative` — 1+3

**Design thesis:** use KOKUBO-like blue/white editorial discipline as the base and Nike-like cyan/lime material energy as the accent.

- Palette: warm white, cobalt blue, transparent cyan, electric lime. Use full cobalt fields sparingly for pacing.
- Type: bold clean sans headlines with small blue editorial labels.
- Grid: clear asymmetrical stage; let one generated object occupy 45–65% of the slide.
- Media: generate studio still lifes from folded white paper, cobalt geometry, translucent acrylic, cyan refraction, and lime light.
- Icons: original geometric line icons in blue/cyan/lime, each centered in a pale circular module.
- Page silhouettes:
  - `prism-cover`: left title block, right abstract translucent studio composition.
  - `object-text-split`: one sculptural object beside a three-step argument.
  - `blue-data-field`: cobalt field with three large stages and cyan/lime progress marks.
  - `icon-process`: four semantic circular icons connected by blue lines.
- Avoid pastel blobs, cute stickers, coral/mint card grids, and miscellaneous decorative shapes.

## `academic-report` — 3+4

**Design thesis:** combine research-paper hierarchy with high-saturation blue and scientific visual models so rigor stays legible and contemporary.

- Palette: saturated research blue, warm white, pale blue modules, coral evidence markers.
- Type: precise sans hierarchy; use numbered method labels and compact explanatory captions.
- Grid: modular research-paper layout with a persistent evidence spine or method sequence.
- Media: scientific isometric/3D models with blue measurement layers and coral signal overlays; label the conceptual status of generated visuals.
- Icons: semantic research icons in pale circles, joined by blue connectors.
- Page silhouettes:
  - `research-hero`: blue title panel plus a large scientific model.
  - `method-chain`: four method stages with editable labels and icons.
  - `evidence-sidebar`: blue result/metric sidebar plus a large editable chart.
  - `model-implications`: model image occupying two-thirds, implications list at right.
- Citation rule: never invent sources; keep slide-level citations compact and put complete references on a sources slide.
- Avoid sepia thesis styling, long paragraphs, ornamental serifs, and unlabeled scientific imagery.

## `editorial-premium` — 2+5

**Design thesis:** create premium tension through architectural grids, black-and-white avant-garde photography, scale, crop, and silence—not black-and-gold decoration.

- Palette: warm editorial paper, black, one vermilion accent.
- Type: oversized grotesk headlines, occasional italic red word, tiny issue/index metadata.
- Grid: strict architectural columns made visible through hairlines and repeated alignments.
- Media: high-contrast monochrome architecture, sculptural fashion, or object studies with hard directional light.
- Icons: normally none. Prefer issue numbers, crop marks, and hairline rules.
- Page silhouettes:
  - `photographic-cover`: full-bleed monochrome photo with large black headline and tiny metadata.
  - `typographic-grid`: no image; one oversized statement crossing a visible grid.
  - `fashion-split`: large photo paired with a compact editorial text column.
  - `object-editorial`: two-thirds object photograph plus numbered principles and a red baseline.
- Avoid gold gradients, luxury clichés, serif wedding aesthetics, rounded cards, and photo collages.
